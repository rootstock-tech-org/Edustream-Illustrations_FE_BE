What SMART-SEG is
A simulation of an industrial waste-segregation line for NTPC. Items ride a conveyor past a sensor array, an AI classifier reads only what the sensors observed, and a diverter gate routes each item to RDF or to reject. Everything on this page describes the modelled physical installation — the pages under Monitoring show it running.

What happens to one item
{{ cfg.TICK_RATE_HZ }} Hz loop

  1. Spawn

    An item enters at the head of the belt with a true material type, mass and dimensions drawn from the material table. This is ground truth, and nothing downstream of segmentation is allowed to see it.

  2. Travel

    Position advances by speed × dt each tick. Belt speed and width come from Line Control on the Overview.

  3. Sense

    The array renders this frame's raw signals — a depth frame, an NIR line, a scalar mass, an inductive strength — each with modelled noise.

  4. Segment & track

    OpenCV segments the depth frame into objects and assigns stable track IDs. From here on the pipeline sees tracks, never items.

  5. Accumulate

    While a track sits over a sensor it collects readings. Features are the latched maxima of what was observed.

  6. Classify

    Once, at {{ cfg.SCAN_ZONE_START_M + cfg.SCAN_ZONE_LENGTH_M }} m, the Random Forest reads the eight-feature vector and the failsafes get a veto. Below {{ (cfg.CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD * 100)|int }}% confidence the decision is flagged for review but not overridden.

  7. Divert

    At {{ cfg.DIVERTER_GATE_POS_M }} m the gate routes the item. Only here is the track matched back to the physical item — by xy-proximity — to actuate and to score accuracy.

Two decision layers

The Random Forest runs first, then hard-coded failsafes can override it. They sit ahead of the model's output rather than merged into it, so a hedging model cannot pass a dense or metallic object:

  • Density above {{ cfg.DENSITY_HAZARD_THRESHOLD }} kg/m³ with C-H absorption below {{ cfg.NIR_LOW_THRESHOLD }} → HAZARD — dense with no polymer signature is stone or concrete.
  • Metal detected above {{ cfg.MASS_HEAVY_THRESHOLD }} kg → HAZARD — heavy metal damages the shredder downstream.
  • Confidence below {{ (cfg.CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD * 100)|int }}% → flagged, decision left as the model made it.
